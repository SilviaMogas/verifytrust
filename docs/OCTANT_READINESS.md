## Submission blockers (must clear before calling the project Octant-ready)

1. **Repository visibility** — private until Silvia approves making it public.
2. **Hosted demo URL** — `/demo` and `/octant` need a public deployment with a
   configured relayer key and RPC.

## Non-blocking gaps (state openly in the application)

- Demo issuer sees the customer secret (blind issuance = future work).
- Review commitment not bound inside the proof.
- Timestamp not range-checked; no eligibility windows.
- Barretenberg nightly build; no external audit.
